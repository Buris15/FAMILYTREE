Dr. Juanita Valdivia Lacsamana, Ed.D. (affectionately known as "Tetang") was a prominent educator, academic administrator, and devout Catholic lay leader in La Union. Born on March 8, 1917, in Alaminos, Pangasinan, she lived to become a centenarian, celebrating her 100th birthday in 2017.


Career in Education and Public Service

• Basic Education Leadership. She began her career as a classroom teacher and later became the Principal of Gabaldon Elementary School in San Fernando, La Union.
• Advanced Degrees. While working, she earned her Master of Arts degree in Dagupan. In 1976, she completed her Doctor of Education (Ed.D.) at Saint Louis University in Baguio City.
• Department of Education. Following her graduate studies, she was promoted to District Supervisor and subsequently served as General Education Supervisor I for English in the Department of Education in La Union until her mandatory retirement in 1982 at age 65.
• Higher Education and Post-Retirement. Retirement did not slow down her work in education:
	• She taught master's and doctoral students at Lorma College, Saint Louis College, and Don Mariano Marcos Memorial State University (DMMMSU).
	• She served as Dean of the Graduate School at Osias Educational Foundation in Balaoan, La Union.
	• From 1989 to 1996, she capped off her formal educational career as Principal of St. Jude Montessori Educational Center at St. Jude Parish.
• Local Governance. She briefly served in local government as an appointed member of the Sangguniang Bayan of the Municipality of San Fernando from 1987 to 1988.

Faith, Community, and Longevity

• Church Leadership. Deeply devoted to the Catholic Church, she attended daily Holy Mass alongside her husband Pidong. She served as President of the Diocesan Catholic Women's League (CWL) from 1980 to 1985 and President of the St. William Cathedral Parish Council from 1983 to 1985.
• Secret to Longevity. Recognized with a Longevity Special Award at 100 years old, her family credited her vitality to a joyful disposition, strong family bonds, daily rosary devotion, and a strict diet of nutritious food prepared completely without salt or bagoong (fermented fish paste), allowing her to live without daily maintenance medications.
