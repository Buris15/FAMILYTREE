V17 FAMILY RESEARCH SCANS
=========================

These five PNG files were supplied with the family research update dated 16 November 2026. The Alfredo Lacsamana / Juanita Valdivia-Valdris item is a register-page scan (page 71), not a standalone marriage contract. The other four are marriage-contract scans.

The source scans are preserved as supplied. Names and ages with discrepancies are described in the website notes rather than silently reconciled:
- Juanita: Valdivia in the register / family biography; Valdris in earlier index notes. The register age also differs slightly from the supplied birth date.
- Concepcion's age differs between the supplied earlier index and the 1955 contract; Prisea / Prinsea is retained as a spelling variant.
- Francisco's contract names the Office of the Justice of the Peace in San Fernando; Joaquin's contract names the Immaculate Conception Church.
- Eduardo's 1959 contract records age 36 years 11 months, which conflicts with an earlier estimate from the index summary.
- Macario's parents are kept in the separate Leon / Bulacan–Manila record group, not merged into Don Leon's La Union branch.

PRIVACY: the scans contain personal details, handwritten signatures and address information. They are in the public/ directory and will be directly accessible to website visitors after deployment. Do not deploy the scans publicly until you have permission to share them; redact signatures, addresses and any details that living people have not agreed to publish.
